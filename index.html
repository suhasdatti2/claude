About Smiling Sprouts

Where every child blooms at their own pace.

Smiling Sprouts was founded with a simple belief: children thrive when they feel safe, seen, and celebrated. Our home-based daycare blends the comfort of a cozy home with the structure of an enriching learning environment.

From morning circle time to creative play, every part of our day is thoughtfully designed to support social, emotional, physical, and cognitive growth.

  • Nurturing, low-ratio classroom
  • Daily activities that build curiosity
  • Open communication with parents
  • Healthy meals & snack options

A Day at Smiling Sprouts

Daily Schedule

A structured yet flexible day designed to balance learning, play, and rest so your child feels happy and secure.

  1. 🕘9:00 – 10:00 AM

    Drop-off & Free Playtime

    A warm welcome and gentle transition with toys and friends.

  2. 🕙10:00 – 11:00 AM

    Circle Time

    Songs, calendar, weather, and a guided learning theme of the day.

  3. 🕚11:00 – 11:30 AM

    Motor Skills Activity

    Movement games and exercises to build coordination and strength.

  4. 🕦11:30 AM – 12:00 PM

    Book Reading & Dancing Time

    Storytime adventures followed by joyful music and movement.

  5. 🕛12:00 – 1:00 PM

    Lunch Time

    A healthy, balanced meal shared together at the table.

  6. 🕐1:00 – 3:00 PM

    Nap Time

    Quiet, cozy rest to recharge little bodies and minds.

  7. 🕒3:00 – 4:00 PM

    Snack Time

    A wholesome snack and friendly conversation.

  8. 🕓4:00 – 5:00 PM

    Free Playtime & Pickup

    Open play and a happy goodbye to family at the door.

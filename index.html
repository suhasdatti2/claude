Our story

A downtown Bellevue tradition.

Perched on the second floor of Lincoln Square South — with sweeping views of Bellevue Way — Duke's has been pouring drinks, shucking oysters, and serving the Northwest's finest wild seafood for over four decades.

Our commitment is simple: 100% sustainable, antibiotic-free, wild-caught seafood, paired with grass-fed Royal Ranch beef and produce from growers we trust. From our award-winning chowder to Chef Bill's seasonal specials, every plate is crafted with intention.

Pull up to the oyster bar, gather the table for a birthday, or duck in for happy hour. You'll leave promising yourself you'll be back soon.

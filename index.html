Why Nick Jr's

Two Kitchens, One Obsession: Flavor

We grill American classics and Greek street food under one roof — both done with the same care, the same fresh ingredients, and zero shortcuts.

Hand-Pressed Smash Burgers

Fresh, never-frozen Angus beef smashed to order on a screaming-hot flat top for that legendary crispy, caramelized edge.

Gyros Carved Fresh

Our Story

A Family Grill With a Greek Soul

Nick Jr. learned to cook in his father's kitchen, where every gyro was carved by hand and every burger got the time it deserved. Today we still do it the same way — recipes passed down, ingredients chosen daily, and a counter where regulars are greeted by name.

  • Locally sourced produce and fresh-baked pita & buns
  • Halal-friendly gyro meat and vegetarian options
  • Catering for parties, offices, and game days

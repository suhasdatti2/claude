Our Story

One family, two traditions, and a whole lot of flavor — here's how Nick Jr's came to be.

From the Old Country to Maple Avenue

It Started With Nick Sr.'s Spit & a Cast-Iron Griddle

Nick Sr. came to Riverside with two things: a treasured family gyro recipe and an unshakable belief that good food brings people together. He opened a tiny lunch counter, carving gyros by hand and flipping burgers on a well-worn griddle.

His son, Nick Jr., grew up between those two grills. By the time he took over, he had a vision: keep his father's Greek classics exactly as they were, and perfect the all-American smash burger right beside them. In 2009, Nick Jr's Burgers & Gyros was born.

Today it's still a family affair. Same recipes. Same hand-carved gyros. Same crispy-edged smash burgers. And the same belief that nobody should ever leave hungry.
